Privacy Policy

Stand Fast: Survival Arena · Effective 22 August 2026

Mistvorn Games ("we", "us", "our") publishes Stand Fast: Survival Arena (the "Game"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the Game collects and how it is used.

Summary

The Game does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. It has no user accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no third-party SDKs. All saved data stays on your device.

Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. The Game does not require an account, an email address, or any other personally identifiable information to play.

Data Stored on Your Device

The Game saves your best survival stats (such as survival time and kill count) locally on your device so they persist between play sessions. This data is never transmitted anywhere — it stays on your device and is only readable by the Game itself.

Third-Party Services

The Game does not currently integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting services. If this changes in a future update (for example, to add analytics or ads), this Privacy Policy will be updated first to disclose exactly what is added and what data it collects.

Data Deletion

Because all data is stored locally, you can delete it at any time by clearing the Game's app data or uninstalling the Game from your device.

Children's Privacy

The Game does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children. Since no personal information is collected at all, none is collected from children either.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example if the Game's functionality changes. The "Effective" date above will be updated whenever this policy changes, and material changes will be reflected here before they take effect.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to privacy@mistvorn.games.
